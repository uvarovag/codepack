Avatar. size="xxl"|"l"|"m"|"s"|"fit". name="Имя Фамилия" shows initials (no url needed). status="active"|"inactive". isScalable grows avatar on hover. a11y: role, tabIndex, aria-label (name covers aria-label); statusLabels for status voice-over, default {active:'Активен', inactive:'Неактивен'}.
